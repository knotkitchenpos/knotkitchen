Shipping Policy

Effective Date: 1st September, 2026

This Shipping Policy applies to physical products and hardware supplied by KnotKitchen.

1. Shipping Availability

KnotKitchen may ship eligible hardware products to serviceable locations within India.

Shipping availability may depend on the delivery address, product availability, logistics partners, and other operational factors.

2. Processing Time

Orders are generally processed after successful payment and verification.

Estimated processing and delivery timelines may vary depending on product availability, destination, courier service, and other circumstances.

Any estimated delivery date provided by KnotKitchen is an estimate and is not guaranteed unless expressly stated otherwise.

3. Delivery

Orders may be delivered through third-party courier and logistics providers.

Customers are responsible for providing an accurate and complete delivery address and contact information.

4. Delivery Delays

Delivery may be delayed due to circumstances including:

  • Courier or logistics issues
  • Incorrect or incomplete address information
  • Weather or natural events
  • Government restrictions
  • Regional service disruptions
  • High-demand periods
  • Other circumstances beyond KnotKitchen's reasonable control

5. Damaged Packages

If a package appears visibly damaged at the time of delivery, customers should document the condition and contact KnotKitchen support as soon as possible.

Additional information or photographs may be requested to process the claim.

6. Shipping Charges

Applicable shipping charges, if any, will be communicated during the order or purchase process.
