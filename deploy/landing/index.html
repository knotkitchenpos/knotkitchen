Pricing

One POS plan.
Add only what you need.

The POS plan, add-ons and tablets are prepaid from your KnotKitchen wallet; printers are paid once, online. Standard prices per outlet in Indian Rupees (INR); the app shows the exact amount before you pay. No installation fee.

POS plan

₹399 + GST / 30 days

  • Billing, receipts and KOTs
  • Takeaway and delivery orders
  • Menu management
  • Sales reports for any date range
  • Staff accounts with roles

Start by recharging your wallet with ₹2,500 or more in one payment: ₹399 + GST is taken from it and the rest stays in your wallet. The plan, add-ons and tablets then renew together from the wallet every 30 days.

If the balance can’t cover a renewal, or runs out, you have 24 hours to recharge before the POS locks. Billing stays open, and the POS unlocks as soon as you pay.

Add-ons

Switch on what you need. Added mid-period, you pay only for the days left.

  • QR Table OrderingTables and dine-in service, with a QR on each table so diners order from their phone.
    ₹200+ GST / 30 days
  • WebsiteYour own ordering website and table booking, with online payments (also at QR tables) into your own Cashfree or PhonePe account.

Tablets

₹600 + GST / 30 days

Rental; each extra tablet ₹500 + GST / 30 days. Each tablet needs its own wallet top-up of ₹4,000 or more in one payment, made after your POS plan has started (the first ₹2,500 recharge doesn’t count). The money stays in your wallet and pays your bills.

Delivered and set up by KnotKitchen at serviceable locations in India. The tablet stays KnotKitchen’s property: to end the rental, contact support and return it.

Receipt printers

  • 2-inch receipt printer
    ₹1,900incl. GST, one time
  • 3-inch receipt printer
    ₹4,250incl. GST, one time

Buy once, paid online by UPI, card or netbanking (not from your wallet). No monthly fee. Cancel before KnotKitchen accepts the order for a full refund to your wallet.

Other charges

  • Online-paid orders: ₹9 + GST per order paid online through your website or table QR, from the start date shown in the app.
  • E-bills (digital receipts sent to customers by SMS/WhatsApp): ₹0.25 + GST per e-bill sent. Failed sends are free.
  • GST, where applicable, is shown separately on your invoice. Printer prices already include it.
  • Delivery and returns of tablets and printers: see the Shipping and Return policies.

Cancellation & refunds

Cancel anytime from Billing in the app or by email; it takes effect at the end of the paid period. Your wallet balance is not refundable, except as the Refund & Cancellation Policy states (for example, duplicate or erroneous payments).

To start, recharge your KnotKitchen wallet with ₹2,500 or more in one payment. The POS plan (₹399 + GST per 30 days) is paid from it and the rest stays in your wallet.
