Features

Pricing

One POS plan.
Add only what you need.

The POS plan and add-ons are prepaid from your KnotKitchen wallet; tablets and printers are bought once, online. Standard prices per outlet in Indian Rupees (INR); the app shows the exact amount before you pay. No installation fee.

POS plan

₹499 + GST / 30 days

  • Billing, receipts and KOTs
  • Takeaway and delivery orders
  • Menu management
  • Sales reports for any date range
  • Staff accounts with roles

Start by recharging your wallet with ₹3,000 or more in one payment: ₹499 + GST is taken from it and the rest stays in your wallet. The plan and your 30-day add-ons then renew together from the wallet every 30 days. After that, each top-up is ₹1,000 or more.

If the balance can’t cover a renewal, or runs out, you have 24 hours to recharge before the POS locks. While it is locked, staff can’t take orders or settle bills, and your website and table QR stop taking new orders. The owner can still sign in and recharge in Settings → Billing & Subscription, and everything unlocks as soon as the payment lands.

Other charges
  • Platform fee on orders paid online: ₹3 + GST per website order and ₹1 + GST per table bill paid by QR. It is added to your customer’s bill as a “Platform fee” line only when they pay online, and the same amount is then deducted from your wallet. No fee on cash or counter payments, and it comes back to your wallet if the order is cancelled.
  • Knot Eats (optional listing on eats.knotkitchen.com): ₹9 + GST per Knot Eats order (charged to the customer, passed through your wallet). It is that order’s Platform fee in place of the website fee, and it comes back to your wallet if the order is cancelled.
  • E-bills (digital receipts sent to customers by SMS/WhatsApp): ₹0.25 + GST per bill. Failed sends and re-sends of the same bill are free.
  • GST, where applicable, is shown separately on your invoice. Tablet and printer prices already include it.
  • Delivery and returns of tablets and printers: see the Shipping and Return policies.
