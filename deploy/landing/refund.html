Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: 3rd October, 2026

This Refund & Cancellation Policy explains the conditions under which customers may cancel KnotKitchen services or request a refund.

1. Subscription Cancellation

A restaurant may cancel its KnotKitchen POS subscription at any time, by itself in the KnotKitchen POS (Billing, owner only) or by written notice to support@knotkitchen.com.

Renewals stop at the end of the POS Plan's current 30-day billing period, and the service keeps working until then. When the cancellation takes effect, the subscription ends and the restaurant's store is marked Closed. Until then, the cancellation can be undone. The account is permanently deleted only if the restaurant asks, and legal and tax records are kept for the period the law requires.

2. The KnotKitchen Wallet Is Not Refundable

KnotKitchen charges (the POS Plan, add-ons, rentals of tablets rented before 3 October 2026, platform fees on orders paid online and e-bill charges) are prepaid from the KnotKitchen Wallet. The Wallet can be used only for KnotKitchen charges; it is not transferable and earns no interest.

Wallet balance is not refundable, and unused balance is not paid out on cancellation or closure, except:

  • a duplicate or erroneous payment (see section 5);
  • where KnotKitchen terminates the service for its own convenience or because of its own breach, in which case the unused balance, and the fees paid for the unused part of the current period, are refunded by bank transfer;
  • a refund for a hardware order that KnotKitchen cancelled, which is paid out by bank transfer on request (see section 4); and
  • where applicable law requires a refund.

A platform fee deducted from the Wallet for an order a customer paid online is credited back to the Wallet if that order is cancelled or rejected.

3. Plan and Add-on Fees

POS Plan and add-on fees for a billing period that has started are not refunded. This includes the fee for an add-on billed yearly (such as the Website add-on) for a year that has started, also when the store closes before that year ends. An add-on that is stopped keeps working until the end of the period already paid.

4. Hardware Orders

A hardware order (tablet or printer) cancelled before KnotKitchen accepts it is refunded in full to the KnotKitchen Wallet. Once KnotKitchen has accepted the order, it can no longer be cancelled by the customer.

If KnotKitchen cancels a hardware order that has not been delivered, everything paid for it is refunded in full to the KnotKitchen Wallet, unless the cancellation was caused by the customer (for example, a refused delivery, a wrong address or contact, or a breach of the agreement), in which case the documented costs KnotKitchen actually incurred may be deducted. On request, KnotKitchen pays that refund out by bank transfer instead, to the extent it is still in the Wallet.

Tablets and printers bought from KnotKitchen: KnotKitchen replaces a tablet or printer that is dead on arrival or becomes faulty within 7 days of delivery. If KnotKitchen cannot supply a replacement within 15 days of the report, the price is refunded to the original payment method. After those 7 days, the manufacturer's warranty applies, and KnotKitchen will help the customer make the warranty claim.

Tablets rented before 3 October 2026 remain KnotKitchen's property. Returns and loss or damage are covered by the Return Policy.

5. Failed, Duplicate or Erroneous Payments

If an amount is successfully debited from a customer's account due to a failed or duplicate payment to KnotKitchen, KnotKitchen will investigate the transaction.

A failed, duplicate or erroneous payment made to KnotKitchen is refunded to the original payment method. An amount wrongly deducted from the Wallet is credited back to the Wallet.

6. Refund Processing Time

Once a refund is approved, the time taken for the amount to appear in the customer's account may depend on the payment gateway, bank, card issuer, or other financial institution.

7. Service Termination Due to Violation

No refund may be provided for services suspended or terminated because of fraud, misuse, violation of these Terms, or other prohibited activities, subject to applicable law.

8. How to Request a Refund or Cancellation

To request a cancellation or refund, contact:

KnotKitchen
Email: support@knotkitchen.com
Phone: +91 8062177510

Please include your registered account details, invoice/order number, and reason for the request.

Each request will be reviewed according to the applicable policy and circumstances.

9. Policy Changes

KnotKitchen may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will be published on the KnotKitchen website.
